Understanding Your Estimate

The estimated market value is the model's most likely result based on weighted comparable sales. The likely selling range reflects the uncertainty in those comps.

  • High confidence: Several closely matched, consistent recent sales
  • Moderate confidence: Useful comps with some differences or price variation
  • Limited confidence: Fewer close matches or unusually wide variation among comparable sales

Condition, lot characteristics, basement finish, garages, and major improvements can materially affect value. A personalized CMA can account for details the estimator cannot see.

Compensation Structured Around You

Compensation rates are negotiable. You have control over how much you pay in real estate compensation. When you enter into a listing agreement with your real estate agent, that agreement specifies the total compensation you're willing to pay and how much of that goes to the buyer's agent. This means you can work with your agent to structure compensations in a way that works for your situation, whether that's offering a competitive buyer's agent percentage or adjusting your listing-side compensation. Every transaction is unique, and your agreement should reflect your specific needs.
